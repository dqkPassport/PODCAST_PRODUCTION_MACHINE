"You know... they say deep within the Folded Mountains, there’s a place that doesn't just listen. It remembers. People call it the Hidden Valley.

The legend is simple: the valley doesn’t return your voice... it returns your heart.

(Brief Pause)

She looked down into that vast, green silence, and for some reason, the quiet made her angry. It felt like the world was ignoring her. So, she took a deep breath, leaned over the ridge, and screamed: 'Who are you?!'"
