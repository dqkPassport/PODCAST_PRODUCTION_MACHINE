Let’s be honest: most of us start our day feeling rushed. The alarm goes off, we grab our phones, and immediately we see emails, news, and social media. Before we even brush our teeth, our brains are already stressed. We start worrying about meetings or comparing our lives to other people.

But what if you could take back that first hour? Your morning isn't just about finishing work; it’s about how you feel. Happiness is not a lucky accident. It is something you build with small, daily choices.

Today on Passport Motivation, we are looking at five morning habits. Science shows these habits help you feel happier, lower your stress, and help you start the day your way.<break time="2s" />

Habit 1. Practice Mindfulness
This just means having a "quiet time" before the busy day starts. Instead of looking at your phone, sit quietly for five minutes. You can sit on your bed or a chair. Close your eyes and just feel your breath. This simple practice calms your brain and helps you control your thoughts instead of letting your thoughts control you.

Habit 2. Write Down 3 Things You Are Thankful For
Our brains often focus on the bad things first. We have to teach our brains to see the good things. Get a notebook and write down three specific things you like. It could be the warm sun or the smell of your coffee. When you feel thankful, your brain starts looking for good opportunities instead of problems.<break time="2s" />

Habit 3. Move Your Body
Moving is the fastest way to change how you feel. Even five minutes of movement—like stretching or walking in place—creates "happy chemicals" in your brain. These chemicals make you feel better naturally. By moving early, you tell your brain: "I have energy and I am ready for today!"<break time="2s" />

Habit 4. Drink Water Before Coffee
After sleeping for eight hours, your body needs water. Many people drink coffee first, but coffee on an empty stomach can make you feel more stressed. Make a simple rule: Drink a large glass of water before you touch your coffee. It wakes up your body and helps your brain think clearly.<break time="2s" />

Habit 5. Get 10 Minutes of Natural Light
Your body has an internal clock that controls your mood. To set this clock, your eyes need to see natural light in the morning. Step outside for ten minutes. This helps your body create "serotonin"—a hormone that makes you feel good. It also helps you sleep better at night!<break time="2s" />

Happiness is not a place you arrive at; it is a way of living. You don’t have to do all five habits tomorrow. Just pick one. Do it for one week, then add another one. In one month, you won't just be "surviving" your mornings—you will be enjoying them.<break time="2s" />
